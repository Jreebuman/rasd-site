Privacy & data

Last updated October 2026.

What this website collects

This website does not have a sign-up form and does not set tracking or advertising cookies. When you click "Book a free demo," it opens a WhatsApp or email conversation with us directly — we only see what you choose to send there.

If you become a client

If you sign up for the product, the system stores the operational data needed to run it: orders, visit times, customer names and phone numbers, and similar records your team enters. A few rules we hold ourselves to:

  • It's your data. We don't sell it, share it with other clients, or use it to train anything.
  • We never ask for your customers' or team's passwords. Any connection to your other systems uses an access key you create and can switch off at any time.
  • You can export everything as a spreadsheet whenever you ask, and we delete it from our systems if you close your account.
  • Each client's data is kept separate from every other client's.
